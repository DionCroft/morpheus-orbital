# Morpheus ORBITAL prototype checklist

Use this checklist with the [v0.1.0-prototype release](https://github.com/DionCroft/morpheus-orbital/releases/tag/v0.1.0-prototype), [KiCad project](../circuit_design/Morpheus/Morpheus.kicad_pro), [schematic PDF](Morpheus-Schematic.pdf), and [reference BOM](../Morpheus-BOM.csv). The release contains reviewed CAD. The checks below are open until you record evidence for the exact board revision, fitted parts, and intended fabrication process.

## Before placing a JLCPCB order

### Freeze the prototype and its parts

- [ ] **Pin the revision.** Record the Git tag or commit, the KiCad version, and the BOM revision. Decide whether the order is bare boards or front-side assembly, and record any parts to be fitted by hand.
- [ ] **Re-run native KiCad ERC and DRC** for the files that will be exported. Refill zones and review every new or changed finding. Keep the raw reports and the disposition of each remaining finding. Check connector pin numbering, footprint revisions, polarity, and pin 1 against the exact parts.
- [ ] **Confirm the orderable parts.** Check the exact MPNs and any JLCPCB/LCSC part numbers against the package drawing and pinout. Review any proposed substitute for electrical ratings, package, footprint, and orientation. Scope the assembly BOM and placement list to the parts the assembler will fit. Treat U5 as off-board, C47/R23/R24/R31 as do-not-populate, and NT901 as copper. Record how the through-hole headers will be assembled.
- [ ] **Confirm the display and battery interfaces.** For the Good Display GDEP073E01 and Hirose FH34SRJ-50S-0.5SH(50) at J3, verify the actual FPC revision, contact side, pin 1, stiffener, tail dimensions, latch access, and permitted fold. Verify USB-C plug and microSD card clearance, enclosure and fastener clearance, and the ESP32-S3 antenna keepout. For the intended Jauch LP675365JU/246522 pack, obtain the exact revision's protection, charging limits, lead polarity, harness and thermistor details. [Hirose connector drawings](https://www.hirose.com/en/product/p/CL0580-1266-2-50) · [Espressif antenna guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/pcb-layout-design.html)
- [ ] **Set test limits before ordering.** Record the rail, current, temperature, and timing limits for the exact parts. Resolve power, charging and display uncertainties that prevent a safe first-power plan. For the e-paper panel, obtain the correct controller, waveform, BS0 interface mode, and power-up/down sequence. Keep any unqualified operating mode out of the first bring-up plan.

### Confirm the factory process and files

- [ ] **Confirm the stackup and USB impedance in writing.** The provisional target is six layers, nominal 1.6 mm, using JLC06161H-7628. Confirm finished thickness and tolerance, dielectric and copper thicknesses, solder mask, finish, and copper etch assumptions. Request a **90 Ω differential, ±10%** USB review for the actual front and back routing and return planes. Include launches, widened gaps, and through-via transitions in the review. Record the agreed trace geometry and any coupon or measurement plan. [JLCPCB stackup table](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance) · [Espressif USB layout guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/pcb-layout-design.html)
- [ ] **Confirm selective via treatment with JLCPCB.** This CAD revision has 434 through vias with a 0.30 mm CAD drill and 0.60 mm land. The 61 specified vias need fill and copper capping. Of those, 28 have front tenting disabled and 33 are tented on both sides in CAD. The other 373 are ordinary tented vias. Provide an annotated location list or map registered to the fabrication drill origin, with the groups clearly identified. All groups share a drill size. Obtain confirmation of the exact selection, fill material, plating and flatness, front and back mask treatment, finish, and any stencil implications. [JLCPCB via covering options](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering) · [JLCPCB filled-and-capped via guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/via-in-pad-pofv-guide)
- [ ] **Generate and independently inspect fabrication outputs.** From the pinned PCB, export Gerbers and plated/non-plated drill data. Open the actual output set in a Gerber viewer and check the closed outline, layer order and registration, drills, all six copper layers, mask, silkscreen and any stencil layers. Review openings around J3 and exposed pads, visible pin-1 markers, and the rear silkscreen.
- [ ] **Verify assembly outputs, if ordering assembly.** Export the assembly BOM and CPL from the same revision. Match designators in both files and record through-hole or hand-fitted parts separately. Check origins and units, side, footprint centre, polarity and rotation against the actual JLCPCB placement preview. Inspect the display connector, USB-C, ICs and polarised parts individually. [JLCPCB KiCad BOM/CPL guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-generate-the-bom-and-centroid-file-from-kicad)
- [ ] **Approve the factory preview.** Check the actual quotation and DFM feedback against the layer stack, USB impedance, via map and selected assembly parts. Review the final production and placement previews before paying or approving production. If the factory proposes a geometry, layer, process or part change, review the affected design checks and exports again.

## When the board arrives

### 1. Inspect it unpowered

- [ ] Match the board revision, dimensions, finish, assembly and factory reports to the order. Inspect connector pins, IC/diode orientation, solder bridges, exposed-pad areas, damaged components and visible via finish under magnification.
- [ ] With the panel and battery disconnected, check supply-to-ground resistance and continuity on expected connections. Investigate unexplained low resistance, crossed power nets or incorrect connector pinout before powering.
- [ ] Dry-fit connectors and the enclosure with power off. Check plug and card access, the panel tail insertion and fold without stressing the latch, battery polarity and strain relief, and antenna clearance in the assembled configuration.

### 2. Bring up power and charging

- [ ] Start with the panel and actual cell disconnected. Use a current-limited USB-C test setup with a known advertised current. Compare input current, regulator outputs and startup transients with the pre-recorded limits.
- [ ] Test one source state at a time. Check default-current PC USB, a USB-C source advertising at least 1.5 A, battery-powered USB, depleted-cell conditions, attach/removal and reset. The intended design requires at least 1.5 A advertised for batteryless or depleted-cell USB startup. Default-current PC USB is intended for programming with a powered battery. Verify that the board respects the advertised current, inhibits unqualified charging and avoids backfeeding.
- [ ] Exercise hardware charge-disable and simulate thermistor open, short, hot and cold conditions. Use a suitable battery simulator that can sink charge current when testing charger behaviour. With the simulator, verify charge voltage, current, termination and fault behaviour before connecting the exact protected cell. Monitor charger, regulator and inductor temperatures under the declared loads. [TI BQ25616 charger datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25616.pdf)

### 3. Bring up interfaces and display

- [ ] Load minimal revision-matched firmware and confirm boot/reset and strap behaviour. Test USB enumeration and disconnect, microSD read/write, BME690 sensing, fuel gauge and ADC readings, buttons and status LEDs. Check expected errors and recovery on unplug, reset and removal of a peripheral.
- [ ] Exercise USB-C in both plug orientations using the intended cables and sources. Confirm that the board selects the expected source state and that enumeration, reconnect and disconnect are consistent. Correlate the VBUS monitor with a measured attach or removal before relying on it in firmware.
- [ ] Bring up the e-paper panel last. Confirm connector orientation and use the exact panel waveform and power sequence. Observe rail sequencing, startup current, bias overshoot, settling, Q8 drain-to-source peaks, and temperature through repeated refresh and shutdown. Verify a bounded BUSY timeout and return to the intended sleep current. Use the actual measured waveform and component ratings to assess margin.
- [ ] Repeat the representative refresh, storage and communications workload with the intended battery, display and enclosure. Measure peak and sleep current, operating temperature and RF performance. Save any ordered impedance coupon results and board-level USB measurements.

**Stop and investigate** if you see unexpected current draw or charging, wrong polarity, rail excursions, oscillation, overheating, failed charge-disable, or any uncertain FPC contact or fold. Save the capture and record the board serial or revision, actual fitted parts, source state, limits, instruments, measured values, and pass/fail for each test.
